How to Play Light Up (Akari)

Light Up (Akari) is a Japanese logic puzzle with simple rules and rich depth:


  • Goal: Place light bulbs on the grid so that every white square is illuminated.
  • Illumination: A bulb casts light horizontally and vertically until blocked by a black square or the grid edge.
  • No Bulb Clashes: No light bulb may shine directly onto another light bulb (no two bulbs can see each other along a straight row or column without a black square between them).
  • Numbered Black Cells: A number in a black square (0, 1, 2, 3, or 4) indicates exactly how many bulbs touch its four orthogonal edges (up, down, left, right).
  • Unnumbered Black Cells: Can have any number of adjacent bulbs (or none).

Controls:
  • Left Click: Place / remove light bulb (or use selected tool).
  • Right Click: Place / remove cross (X) to mark squares where bulbs cannot go.
  • Click & Drag: Quickly mark crosses or erase across multiple cells.
  • Shortcuts: Space / B = Bulb, X = Cross, Z = Undo, Y = Redo, H = Hint.
